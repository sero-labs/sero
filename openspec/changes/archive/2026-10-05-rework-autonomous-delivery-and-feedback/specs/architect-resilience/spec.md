## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Architect owns discovery from limited input

New delivery runs SHALL start from the user's request and essential constraints without a quality selector or predefined solution plan. Architect SHALL determine the needed solution and work just in time, using judgment to choose direct work, investigation, Rooms or Workflows and to revise that choice from findings. A research deliverable MUST NOT be compulsory for an understood task. Any investigation SHALL have a question, stopping condition and bounded allocation within the approved budget. The proving run for this change MUST NOT force a mode, team size, operator-written plan or detailed implementation procedure.

#### Scenario: Architect chooses how to develop the project
- **WHEN** a project starts from a short brief and approved boundaries
- **THEN** Architect chooses its own route to useful work and the evidence records why any research or delegation was needed
- **AND** operator coaching or an operator-supplied plan prevents classification as unassisted delivery

#### Scenario: The core unknown is already resolved
- **WHEN** existing relevant findings answer the active question
- **THEN** Architect uses those findings and proceeds instead of repeating the same investigation

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Research and recovery do not hide delivery

Architect SHALL pursue useful results and checks suited to the actual request without silently weakening user-stated requirements. Pragmatic-first advice SHALL guide its judgment, not force an early-demo milestone, minimum research allocation or fixed solution path. Necessary research, checking and recovery SHALL remain visible through live summaries and detailed work access before completion. A written research report MUST NOT be presented as the requested application. Bounded automatic recovery SHALL proceed without operator instructions when it preserves approved scope, authority and operation identity. An unsafe or exhausted recovery SHALL raise a short actionable hold rather than becoming silently idle.

#### Scenario: MiniSynth's research completes
- **WHEN** a research Room returns an audio architecture brief but no playable application exists
- **THEN** the project identifies it as research, not delivery of MiniSynth
- **AND** eligible implementation continues under the applicable approval rules

#### Scenario: A recoverable check failure
- **WHEN** a current check identifies a local defect within approved scope and recovery limits remain
- **THEN** Architect shows checking or repairing activity, applies a bounded repair and requests fresh verification without asking the user to manage the worker

#### Scenario: Budget is exhausted during repair
- **WHEN** safe repair cannot start within the remaining authority
- **THEN** the project preserves work, identifies the precise hold and asks for the necessary boundary change rather than looping or quietly reducing the outcome

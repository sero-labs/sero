# Sero — Landing Page Copy

## HERO

**Zero context switch.**
**Zero sprawl.**

Sero is an agent-first workspace for macOS. One window. Your code, your terminal, your AI — all in one place, all in flow.

*Available now for Apple Silicon.*

---

## THE PROBLEM

You're not slow. Your setup is.

Every time you alt-tab to a terminal, open a browser to check a preview, switch to a chat window to ask your AI something, then hunt for the file you were just editing — you pay a tax. Not in seconds. In momentum.

The average developer juggles five or more applications to do one thing. That's not a workflow. That's context-switching overhead disguised as productivity.

---

## THE SOLUTION

**Everything you need. One window. One agent.**

Sero organizes your work into projects. Each project keeps its own file tree, editor, terminals, and AI conversation — all instantly accessible from a single sidebar. Nothing gets lost. Nothing needs to be reopened.

The AI agent isn't a chatbot you visit. It lives in a persistent panel beside your work, always aware of what you're doing, ready when you need it.

---

## FEATURES

---

**Your projects. Organized.**

A clean sidebar tree keeps all your workspaces and their sessions at a glance. No hunting for windows. No lost context. Switch projects in one click, return to exactly where you left it.

---

**An agent that's always there.**

The chat panel stays open beside your work — or slides away when you need focus. It streams responses in real time, shows its reasoning when it thinks through hard problems, and visualizes every tool it runs so you always know what's happening. Ask it anything. Hand it a task. It won't forget what you told it last week.

---

**It remembers.**

Sero's memory system persists across every session. It learns your preferences, your stack, your patterns. Before each conversation, it quietly injects what's relevant — facts you've shared, lessons from past sessions, notes you asked it to keep. You stop re-explaining yourself.

---

**Multiple agents. Real collaboration.**

Delegate complex tasks to specialist subagents running in parallel. Watch their live output, token usage, and tool activity as they work. Or run Collaboration mode — four agents debating your problem as Coordinator, Researcher, Analyst, and Visionary — structured disagreement that surfaces better answers.

---

**A real development environment.**

Built-in file explorer, Monaco editor with diff view, and integrated terminals — all inside the same window. Full xterm.js support with 10,000-line scrollback. Every terminal runs inside its own container.

---

**Isolated by default.**

Each project gets its own Linux container, started automatically the first time you use it. Your files are visible inside. Your SSH keys work. Nothing bleeds between projects. No manual Docker setup required.

---

**All your dev servers. Under control.**

A single panel in the status bar shows every running dev server — framework, port, status. Open a preview in your browser, restart a server, kill it — without touching the terminal.

---

**Extend everything.**

Sero's plugin system loads apps at runtime with no restart. Install from npm, GitHub, or a local path. Built-in apps — git integration, admin tools, memory browser, cron jobs — work the same way as anything you build yourself.

---

**Make it yours.**

A live theme editor for colors, typography, and spacing — with handcrafted presets to start from. System light/dark sync. Your workspace should feel like yours.

---

## THE PRINCIPLE

*"The container is the body. The UI is the face. The agent is the mind."*

Sero isn't an editor with AI added on. It isn't a chatbot with a file browser bolted to it. It was designed from the ground up around the idea that your AI agent and your development environment belong together — not in separate windows, not connected by copy-paste, but unified in a single, coherent workspace.

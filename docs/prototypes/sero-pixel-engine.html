Sero Pixel Engine

Sprites made from language and matrices, not from image models. The model writes a grid of palette indexes; a deterministic compiler turns it into exact pixels, sheets and animation data. Every sprite shown here was produced by the real engine spike — the explorer was ingested from a supplied reference, and the walk cycle moves parts of it without redrawing a single pixel.

5 · Sheet and export

Compilation is deterministic and runs no model. The atlas is Aseprite-compatible, so Godot, Unity, Phaser and LÖVE import it without editing, and project.json plays directly in the engine's own clip player with no sheet at all.

6 · Kept sprites

Sprites keep their own surface rather than mixing sheets into a grid of web pages. A kept version is immutable: later edits to the project never change it, and it carries the exact grids, the sheet, the atlas and its provenance.

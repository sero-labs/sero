## Allowed Values Legend

### Product Status

- `built-in/core` — functionality shipped as part of the core desktop/web app rather than a plugin.
- `built-in/plugin` — functionality shipped from an in-repo `plugins/sero-*-plugin/` package.
- `external/local` — functionality found in an external or locally installed plugin outside the Sero monorepo.
- `experimental` — functionality present but not ready to position as stable or generally available.
- `placeholder` — manifest, stub, demo, or incomplete surface that should not be promoted as a complete feature.
- `unclear` — product status cannot be determined from current evidence.

### Verification Status

- `verified` — source paths substantiate the user-facing claim, product status is clear, and caveats are captured.
- `partially verified` — evidence supports part of the claim, but exact scope, UX, configuration, or shipped behavior still needs review.
- `needs verification` — row is normalized from the scout but has not yet been checked against enough source/docs evidence.
- `blocked` — verification cannot proceed until missing access, product decision, credentials, environment, or owner input is available.
- `exclude from public copy` — claim is unsupported, internal-only, sensitive, confusing, placeholder, or otherwise unsafe for docs/marketing.

### Confidence

- `high` — strong evidence and low ambiguity.
- `medium` — plausible evidence with some uncertainty around scope, support status, or user-facing behavior.
- `low` — weak, indirect, manifest-only, stale, or ambiguous evidence.

### Impact

- `High` — likely central to product value, onboarding, or high-priority docs/copy.
- `Medium` — useful feature or supporting workflow worth documenting when relevant.
- `Low` — niche, example, novelty, internal, or later-priority item.

### Audience

Use one or more of: `General user`, `Power user`, `Developer`, `Plugin author`, `Admin`, `Support`, `Plugin user`.

### Output Targets

Use one or more of: `user docs`, `admin docs`, `developer docs`, `plugin docs`, `support`, `website`, `onboarding`, `release notes`, `integration docs`, `plugin examples`, `later`.

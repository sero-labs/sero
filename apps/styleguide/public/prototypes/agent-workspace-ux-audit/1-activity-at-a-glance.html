Activity at a glance

Proposal 1 of 6 for the agent and workspace UX audit. It covers flows A, Triage, and B, State vocabulary. It answers your six activity questions on the lists and at the top of a project, so nobody has to open anything to find out whether work is happening.

The style does not change. It uses the same surfaces, accent, density and controls as proposals.html. Every screen is drawn from real records in the seroarchitectdev profile as they were on 19 September. Under each screen, Controls on this screen today lists every control the shipped screen has and what happens to it.
